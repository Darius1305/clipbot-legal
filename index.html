clipbot

A personal tool that turns Twitch clips into short vertical videos.

clipbot is a private, non-commercial tool operated by a single person. Once or twice a day it collects popular clips of a few Twitch streamers who have agreed to this, edits them into vertical short videos (9:16) and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. Every video credits the streamer and links the original clip.

The app only accesses the operator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It has no other users and does not collect data from viewers.
