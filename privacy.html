Privacy Policy

clipbot · last updated 2 October 2026

Who uses this app

clipbot is used only by its operator to publish videos to the operator's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It has no other users.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos (scope youtube.upload) to the operator's channel.
  • TikTok: basic profile information and permission to upload or post videos to the operator's account.
  • Twitch: public clip information (title, view count, link) through the official Twitch API.

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens are used only to upload the generated videos. They are stored encrypted and are never sold, shared or used for advertising. The app keeps a list of clip IDs it has already used so nothing is posted twice. No data about viewers or other people is collected.

Google API Services

clipbot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting data

Revoking the app's access in your Google or TikTok account settings stops all access. Stored tokens are deleted on request.
